Rankedin

Welcome to Rankedin

Compare yourself with friends across many more days of LinkedIn games than LinkedIn itself shows — an IQ & ELO leaderboard over a shared, ever-growing pool of results.

No account, no passwords. You just install a small tool on your computer and sync your results through your already-logged-in LinkedIn. You only see other people's data once you share your own.

📵 Open this page on a computer (Chrome or Firefox). Sync doesn't work on phones.
Step 2 / 2 · Install

Add your results

  1. 1

    Drag the button up to your bookmarks bar

    If the bar isn't visible in Chrome: ⌘⇧B (Mac) / Ctrl+Shift+B.

    🎮 Rankedin Sync

    Drag this button ↑ up to the bar. Don't click it here.

  2. 2

    Open LinkedIn and log in

    Go to linkedin.com in the same browser, as you normally would.

  3. 3

    Click 🎮 Rankedin Sync in the bar

    A window opens and pulls your results (~a minute). Don't close the LinkedIn tab while it runs. If it stops halfway — just click it again; it resumes where it left off.

🔒 You won't see anyone else's data until you share your own. Only your result per round (number, time) is shared — never your LinkedIn password or cookies.
